# The Swiftie Experience 🎤

A Taylor Swift-themed concert feedback survey developed as part of a school assessment in web development.

**Live Demo:** [The Swiftie Experience](https://lmcl-codes.github.io/survey-form/)

## ✨ Overview

The Swiftie Experience is a fictional concert survey inspired by Taylor Swift's *The Eras Tour*. Designed as an academic assessment, the project simulates a post-concert feedback form for attendees of a fictional concert held at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, Nevada.

The survey allows participants to provide personal information, rate their concert experience, select their favorite Taylor Swift albums, specify their preferred contact method, and share additional feedback.

## 🌷 Features

- **Personal Information Form** – Collects basic attendee information, including name, email address, age, and city address.
- **Concert Satisfaction Rating** – Allows participants to rate their overall concert experience.
- **Favorite Album Selection** – Provides multiple checkbox options featuring Taylor Swift's discography.
- **Preferred Contact Method** – Lets participants choose how they would prefer to be contacted.
- **Additional Feedback** – Includes a comments section for suggestions and other concert-related feedback.
- **Themed User Interface** – Features a Taylor Swift-inspired design and concert-themed content.

| Technology | Purpose |

## 🎨 Design Concept

The website draws inspiration from Taylor Swift's *The Eras Tour*, incorporating concert-related content and a fan-oriented visual theme.

The overall concept aims to make a standard feedback survey more engaging by presenting it through the perspective of a Swiftie attending a concert.

## 📚 Academic Purpose

This website was developed specifically as a **school assessment** to demonstrate foundational web development skills, particularly in creating and styling HTML forms, organizing input elements, and implementing different form controls.

The project provided an opportunity to apply classroom concepts in building a themed and interactive survey interface.
